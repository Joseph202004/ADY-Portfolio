01 — Supply-chain operations · Product design

A control tower for supermarket operations.

less Ops is a prototype I designed for a regional manager looking after 48 stores in Bengaluru. AI agents keep watch on stock, suppliers, shelves and deliveries, and flag problems early. The manager reviews what they suggest and decides what happens.

02 — The problem

Problems slip through because the information is split up.

Stock levels, supplier orders, shelf checks and deliveries all sit in different tools. A shortage might show up in one, the spare stock to fix it in another, and the truck that could move it in a third. Someone has to connect those dots by hand, and by the time they do, the shelf is often already empty.

less Ops brings all four into one place. When something looks off, it opens a case, pulls the related information together and suggests what to do. The manager’s job becomes checking that suggestion, not piecing the picture together.

03 — Who it is for

Built for the people who keep the shelves stocked.

Regional manager. Signs off on anything that costs money or moves stock.

Store and hub staff. Pick, dispatch and receive the goods.

Category and supply teams. Handle suppliers, purchase orders and lead times.

04 — AI agents

How the agents split the work.

One coordinator watches the whole region and hands work to four specialist agents. Each specialist has smaller sub-agents for specific jobs, and they all report back to the coordinator instead of acting on their own.

People make the final call. The agents can suggest a transfer, a purchase order or a delivery, but none of it happens until someone approves it.

05 — System flow

Every case follows the same six steps.

  1. 01

    Detect

    An agent notices something off in the data it’s watching.

    Signal
  2. 02

    Investigate

    The agents dig in and confirm what’s actually going on.

    Evidence
  3. 03

    Recommend

    The coordinator suggests a fix, with its cost and how confident it is.

    Draft
  4. 04

    Approve

    The manager reviews it. Nothing is moved or ordered before this.

    Human
  5. 05

    Execute

    The task goes to the person who will carry it out.

    Action
  6. 06

    Verify

    Someone at the store confirms it’s done, and the case is closed.

    Close
